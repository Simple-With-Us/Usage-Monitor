HTTP/2 sender for the same budget and provider alert pages that already go to Pushover.

## What shipped

- `src/lib/apns.ts` — ES256 provider JWT, `node:http2` transport, injectable mock for tests.
- `alert-delivery` grows an `apns` channel when `APNS_*` is configured and fans out to `ApnsDeviceToken`.
- Dead tokens (410 / 400 BadDeviceToken) are deactivated.  Auth failures do not retry forever.
- Tests never open a socket to Apple.
